# Laurier Sports Insight Society

## Mission Statement

To promote the study of sports strategies using data analytics methods. To provide a repository of information and data pertaining to sports analytics for incoming members. To advance our members' knowledge of data analytics. To share our insights and engage with the general public.

Topics considered include: quantification/visualization of team/player strategies; analysis of injuries or other factors outside of the game and their impact on the play of the game; analysis of the reporting and reaction to sports events.

"Sports" is broadly to mean any competition where strategy and skill are required. For instance, students may wish to analyze chess, game shows, or politics using the methods discussed by the society.

We will specifically avoid any studies where the goal is to gain an advantage in sports betting. 

## Activities

- Student-run tutorials on sports analytics methods and data science in general, resulting in training materials for future members.
- Summaries/discussions of articles about sports analytics.
- Collection and analysis of sports-related data sets as a group.
- Social events, as agreed upon by members.
- Work on and provide support to each other on sports analytics projects/problems
- Compete in sports analytics competitions/hackathons - e.g. NHL Big Data Bowl
- Bring in guest speakers to talk about sports analytics
- Engage with the broader sports analytics community through public-facing events,such as symposia or conferences.
